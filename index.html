Kosmik Circles is an independent collective exploring electronic music, visual experimentation and live experiences. We bring together sound, light and creative practice to build shared spaces, performances and collaborations.

Born from a shared interest in electronic culture, Kosmik Circles moves between DJ sets, audiovisual performance, experimental projects and events.
